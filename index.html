The Practice
Hi everyone, I’m Rinaldi. I’m an architect who loves making music. I created a project called “Desain Nada,” abbreviated as DN, to preserve the melodies I compose. I have always loved creating original melodies, but in the past, I never documented them, and many of them simply disappeared with time. From now on, I want to carefully document and preserve my compositions here, so they can remain as a record of my musical journey.
