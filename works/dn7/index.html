000 Composed Pieces

DN stands for “Desain Nada,” an Indonesian phrase that can be interpreted as “designing music.” The word “Design” is inspired by the world of architecture, while “Nada” comes from the world of music. Desain Nada is the name of a lifelong project that brings together my two worlds, architecture and music.
